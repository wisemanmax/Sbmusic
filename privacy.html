PRIVACY & DATA

What we collect, why, and how to say no.

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This site (Slime By) is run by an independent artist. We try to keep data collection minimal and honest. We do not sell your data, run ad networks, or track you across other websites.

Analytics — only if you accept

The first time you visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics. If you decline (or simply don't choose), we record nothing — no identifier is stored and no events are sent. Only after you press Accept do we begin collecting the data below. You can change your mind any time using the button at the bottom of this page.

What we collect when you accept

DataWhy
Pages you view, links/buttons you click, scroll depth, time on pageTo see which music, pages and features people actually use.
A random anonymous visitor ID & session ID (stored in your browser)To count returning vs new visits without knowing who you are.
Device type (mobile / tablet / desktop), browser & OS familyTo make sure the site works well on what you actually use.
Coarse screen-size bucket, language, timezone, connection typeAudience context — kept as broad buckets, not exact values.
The site (referrer host) you arrived from, on entryTo know where our fans find us.

What we deliberately do NOT collect

  • We do not store your IP address from the website.
  • We do not build a device fingerprint to identify you.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site trackers.
  • The random visitor ID is not linked to your name, email or phone number.

Joining the list (email & SMS)

If you choose to join the slime list, we store the email — and optional phone number — you give us, along with your explicit opt-in, so we can tell you about drops and shows. That's separate from analytics and is the only place we keep contact details. Every message includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete you at any time.

Cookies & local storage

We don't use advertising cookies. The analytics above uses your browser's local storage (small values like a random ID and your consent choice) only after you accept. Clearing your browser storage removes them.

Your choices & rights

You can accept or decline analytics below at any time. Declining stops all tracking and, if you'd previously accepted, no further data is collected. For requests about data we hold from the email/SMS list — access, correction, or deletion — contact us through the connect page.
